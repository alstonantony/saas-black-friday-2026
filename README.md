# SaaS Black Friday

Track 240+ SaaS and AI tools across 14 categories for Black Friday (27 Nov) and Cyber Monday (30 Nov) 2026.

**Website:** [saasblackfriday.online](https://saasblackfriday.online/)

Each tool has its own page with its last recorded offer, full deal history, and an email alert for when the 2026 deal goes live. Offers stay labelled with the year they ran — a 2026 deal is only marked live once it's actually confirmed on the vendor's own site.
